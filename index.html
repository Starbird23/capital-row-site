Why the order matters

The examination happens before the price, not after it.

The price is set twice. Once before the letter of intent is signed, on what everybody believes. And again during due diligence, on what can actually be proved. We move the evidence in front of the first number, so there is only one.

For builders

Run the diligence on your own numbers first

Retention tied to the billing system, the recurring revenue schedule tied to the ledger and the bank, concentration, founder dependency and ownership of the codebase.

What it costs to find out second
For builders

The price becomes a discount

Found after the letter of intent, the same four findings are leverage rather than diligence items, and the leverage has already changed hands. Whatever a buyer’s team would find, you want to be holding it first.

For buyers

Fast-track origination

One hire cannot map a market, and every buyer is chasing the same short list of clean, transactable companies. Coverage puts you in front of qualified targets sooner, with the evidence already run and the end-market read attached.

Does an informed seller cost you?
For buyers

No. A dead deal costs you

Sixty-one per cent of broken letters died on evidence that arrived after the price was set, and every one cost a buyer four months and a diligence budget. A price set on evidence closes. A price set on assertion gets retraded, and a retrade is a coin flip.

The tenth dimension

Nine of the ten things that set the price are already published.

The tenth is ours.

Retention, net retention, churn, recurring share, margin, concentration, founder dependency, sales efficiency and evidence quality. Every competent adviser runs those nine, and every one of them is winnable with time.

10
End-market durability. Who is consolidating your customers’ industry, how fast they are moving, and what they do to the software after a closing. Nobody prices it, because pricing it means knowing the services market as well as the software one. It is the dimension that reprices a deal ninety days after a letter of intent, and it is the reason to call us.
The bench
